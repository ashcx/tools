Media Toolkit

Two browser-local tools for images and audio, each processing files on your device.

Image Compressor

Compress, convert, and resize images in batches.

Formats
JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF and HEIC
Privacy
Images are processed in the browser and are not uploaded.
Caveat
AVIF and HEIC encoding is slower, and the size estimates are guidance, not a guarantee.
Open tool

Audio Toolkit

Process a batch of audio or video soundtracks with one ordered recipe.

Capabilities
Extract, trim, gain, normalize, channels, sample rate, voice or music isolation, speech cleanup
Formats
MP3, AAC/M4A, WAV, FLAC and Opus/OGG
Privacy
Media is processed locally and files are not uploaded.
Caveat
Isolation can take minutes and results vary, and browser support varies for some codecs.
Open tool

No cookies, no analytics, and no third-party requests. The only thing this page stores is your theme choice.
